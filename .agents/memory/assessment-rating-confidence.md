---
name: Assessment rating confidence
description: Why school assessment ratings need both participation-rate and tested-count confidence checks
---

NYC Grades 3–8 ELA/Math result workbooks provide all-grades tested counts but do not provide the eligible-student denominator needed to calculate participation.

**Why:** A participation-only rule cannot identify low-confidence results from these files. Schools with unusually few test takers could otherwise receive a prominent proficiency-driven rating with no warning.

**How to apply:** Use exact participation when an eligible denominator exists; otherwise use the tested-count fallback rather than assuming participation was representative.
